CJB Classics is a professional 9-piece rock and variety band based in Waupaca, Wisconsin. Formerly known as The Justmann Band, we've been entertaining audiences since 1947 — from weddings and festivals to cruise ships and stages across Europe.

We specialize in Classic Rock and Country with incredible variety that will satisfy almost everyone, including Big Band, Polkas, Dixieland, Latin, and Contemporary Rock. Music and Entertainment MAXIMUS!
